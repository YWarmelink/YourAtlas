families + their splitroutes/standalones), 316 not-yet-built ideas from
[`EUROPA_TRIP_IDEAS.md`](EUROPA_TRIP_IDEAS.md) (grouped below by primary country — note: several
Route Builder families still have Dutch names as of this writing, mid-way through the ongoing
Dutch→English translation project, see `README.md`'s Phase 1 status).

**Data-quality note (found while generating this file, 2026-08-19):** `TRIP_DATABASE.csv`'s
`Parent Expedition` field isn't consistently filled in — 36 of the 131 Route Builder routes are
genuine splitroutes/standalones of one of the 13 Grand Trip families (confirmed for several via
this session's translation-batch code recon, e.g. Egypt/Namibia/Kenya/Madagascar/Tanzania/Botswana/
Rwanda/Mauritius/Zimbabwe/Mozambique/Zambia & Malawi/Ethiopia belong to Africa Grand Tour;
Morocco/Sicily/Jordan/Spain/Greece & Crete/Rome & Surroundings/Sardinia/Cyprus/Gulf States Trio/
Malta/Tunisia belong to Mediterranean Civilizations) but are tagged `—` (no parent) in the CSV, so
they appear below as their own single-route group instead of nested under their real family. Not
fixed here — that's a CSV data-entry correction, out of scope for this lightweight index (which
just reflects the CSV as-is). Some `—` entries are legitimately standalone by design (e.g. US
Northeast/Southwest/Hawaii/Florida — built as loose trips, deliberately not merged into North
America Grand Traverse), so not every `—` is a bug.
