## (b) Decisions for Youri

**Car calls**
- **Botswana (3 routes: Botswana, Victoria Falls & Kalahari Loop, Southern Loop):** carInDailyRate false, notes say Central Kalahari needs your own 4x4 or a guided safari. Priced one 4x4: 12 days from Maun × €150 + fuel 920 km (€77) + border tax €30 = €1,907; Kasane/Chobe by bus and game drives (Kasane-Maun bus €25 in local). If you'd rather join a guided mobile safari, the car goes and the guided price is likely higher, not lower (inDailyRate says the safari variant would raise the €175/day).
- **Zambia (Zambia & Malawi, Southern Loop):** the notes describe a self-drive/camping style, so one 4x4: 12 days × €150 + fuel 1,760 km (€174) + COMESA/road tax €35 + one-way drop at Mfuwe/Chipata €150 (est) = €2,159. The €150/day is the reference file's equipped-4x4 rate; shared minibuses/guides would be much cheaper but cover South Luangwa/Lower Zambezi badly. Zambezi guide (€60) is optional.
- **Namibia, South Africa, mountain kingdoms, Angola (loop):** car in the rate, nothing added. Mountain Kingdoms/Loop: Lesotho and Eswatini priced by minibus, not by extending the South African rental (cross-border letter + rental days would add roughly €50 + extra days).
- **No car added (text never mentions one, keyword guess "rental" ignored):** Mauritius (buses €20 local), Zimbabwe (buses €40 + the guided Mana Pools safari), Mozambique (flights). If you do want a rental: Mauritius ≈ 5 days × €30 + fuel ≈ €175, Zimbabwe ≈ 10 days × €120 = €1,200+ (rental restricted).

**Other judgment calls**

- **Ethiopia local €750:** the notes give €400 for flights Addis-Lalibela-Gondar; I added Mekele (Danakil) and Omo return flights (est €350). The whole Amhara/Afar red-advisory text applies; I priced it as written.
