## (b) Decisions for Youri
- **Fares**: 4 searches (SOF, PRG, BUD, TLL); the other airports (BER, VIE, OTP, RIX, VNO, BTS, SKG-out) are estimates scaled from those, low confidence. If real fares are EUR 30-40 cheaper (budget airlines), each route's added drops by that much per leg. Bratislava (BTS) has few direct flights: 120 est; flying into Vienna/Budapest + train would be similar.
- **czechia-austria-9-days**: car 5 days (leave Prague after its days, drop in Vienna; Vienna days car-free), 660 km, one-way cross-border drop fee EUR 100 (est, flagged). Total 382. If you rent all 9 days: +EUR 140; if you take trains instead: about EUR 60 total.
- **czechia-austria-hungary**: car only Prague-Krumlov-Vienna, 3 days, drop fee EUR 100 (est), 400 km = 279; train Vienna-Budapest 25. If trains all the way Prague-Krumlov-Vienna: roughly EUR 60 instead.
- **baltic-states-roadtrip**: car 11 days (from day 3, Tallinn days car-free) x EUR 35, 1460 km, drop fee Tallinn-Vilnius EUR 100 (est), Virtsu-Kuivastu car ferry both ways EUR 48 (est, notes name no price) = 732. Nida eco-toll EUR 30 (midpoint of 10/50, depends on month). If car all 13 days: +EUR 70.
- **hungary-slovenia-croatia**: car 7 days, 1230 km (input figure, probably ~20% high; real loop about 1000 km, -EUR 30 fuel/toll), one-way drop Budapest-Zagreb EUR 120 (est). The notes advise returning the car in the same country: a same-country loop would need a different route. HU vignette 1-day EUR 15.10 is a guess for the shortest product.
- **hungary-roadtrip**: car 4 days (Budapest 2 days car-free), 630 km: 233.
- **moravia-brno**: flown into Prague, train to Brno (EUR 20 return), car 3 days from Brno for the wine region = 129. Notes also suggest cycling: then the car extra (129) disappears. Flying into Vienna was not priced.
- **sofia-rila (5 days)**: mode rental; I priced a 1-day rental (EUR 53). A bus is about EUR 12 return, so the car adds ~EUR 40 over the bus. **sofia-plovdiv-6**: route text says train, so no car.
